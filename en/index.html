Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI operator?

Software that holds a natural phone conversation: understands speech, answers from your knowledge base, and writes outcomes to your CRM. Not a press-1 IVR.

Which language does the AI speak?

Calls run in natural Azerbaijani. The admin dashboard supports AZ, EN, RU, and TR.

Do customers know they are talking to a robot?

Yes — the AI introduces itself as a virtual operator and notes call recording. Opt-outs are blocked automatically.

How large can the knowledge base be?

In the Corporate plan, the AI searches thousands of products and documents in < 1 second without guessing. Other plans include tariffs, rules, and FAQs.

Does the AI answer incoming calls too?

Yes — after hours, on weekends, and when queues overflow. It answers questions, shares department numbers, or logs callback requests.

How much does a minute of conversation cost?

AI ~0.06⁠–⁠0.12 AZN/min + telephony (~0.07 AZN/min), paid directly on your own accounts with per-call cost tracking.

Will it work with our CRM?

Yes, automatically via REST API and webhooks, or without integration via one-click Excel import/export.

What phone line do we need?

Any SIP provider, telecom trunk, or corporate PBX (such as Cisco CUCM).

Where is our data stored?

Strictly on your server: software, database, audio recordings, and transcripts remain in your network.

How long does the launch take?

Typically 2 weeks: scenario setup, server installation, team test calls, and go-live.
