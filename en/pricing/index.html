Pricing questions

Why a one-time payment instead of a subscription?

The software runs on your server and you pay AI minutes directly — zero per-call markup.

How do the packages differ?

By concurrent lines, plus the AI call center (Business & Corporate) and large product catalog search (Corporate).

What if we don’t renew support?

The software keeps running without new updates. The API requires an active “Support with API” plan.

Is VAT included?

No, all prices exclude VAT.

How is the pilot fee counted?

The pilot is 990 AZN; if you purchase a package afterwards, it is deducted in full.
