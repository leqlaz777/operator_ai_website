Security questions

How long are call recordings kept?

You control retention — keep indefinitely or auto-purge after a set number of days.

Is internet access required?

Only outbound to the AI provider and SIP carrier; dashboard and database stay on your LAN.

Can OperatorAI see our data?

No. Everything runs on your server; support connects only when you grant access.
