Questions

Call center questions

Can the AI transfer a call to an agent?

It doesn’t transfer live calls directly: it gives the department number or logs the caller’s name, question and callback time. Routing to the AI is set in your PBX.

Which number do customers call?

Your existing number. Calls reach your PBX or SIP line and route to the AI when needed.

How many calls can the AI answer at once?

100 concurrent calls in Business, and as many as your server and SIP lines allow in Corporate (shared with outbound campaigns).

Can the AI check a customer’s balance or order status?

Live calls don’t read personal databases: general questions get knowledge-base answers, while personal requests are logged for callback.

How big can the knowledge base be?

In Corporate, the AI searches thousands of products and documents in under a second. Other packages include rates, terms, FAQs and branch info.
